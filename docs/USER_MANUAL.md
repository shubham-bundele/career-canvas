# CareerCanvas User Manual

Welcome to **CareerCanvas** -- a free, privacy-first resume builder that runs entirely in your browser. No sign-up required. This guide walks you through every feature so you can create polished, professional documents in minutes.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Opening the App

Open CareerCanvas in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari). Everything loads instantly -- no installation needed.

### The Welcome Wizard

When you create a new document, a step-by-step wizard guides you through setup:

1. **Document Type** -- Choose what you want to create: Resume, CV, Academic CV, Cover Letter, Reference Sheet, LinkedIn Profile Draft, or AI from Job Description.
2. **Career Level** -- Select your experience stage: Student, Graduate, Entry Level, Experienced, Senior, Executive, Career Changer, or Freelancer.
3. **Resume Goal** -- Pick your purpose: General, Tailored to a Job, ATS-Friendly, Visual, or Portfolio.
4. **Page Format** -- Choose A4 (international) or US Letter.

> 💡 **Tip:** Select "AI from Job Description" to paste a job posting and let AI generate a starter resume tailored to that role.

### Dashboard Overview

The Dashboard is your home screen. Here you can:

- **View all documents** as cards showing title, type, template preview, and last-modified time
- **Search** documents by name
- **Filter** by type (Resumes, CVs, Cover Letters, References, Archived)
- **Sort** by last modified, name, creation date, or type
- **Create** a new document with the "+ New Document" button
- **Drag and drop** a file onto the dashboard to import it instantly
- **Duplicate, archive, or delete** documents from each card's menu

## 2. Creating Documents

### Document Types

CareerCanvas supports six document types, each with purpose-built sections and templates:

- **Resume** -- Standard one-to-two page professional resume
- **CV** -- Comprehensive curriculum vitae for detailed career history
- **Academic CV** -- Tailored for academic positions, research, and publications
- **Cover Letter** -- Personalized application letter with recipient and company fields
- **Reference Sheet** -- Formatted list of professional references
- **LinkedIn Profile Draft** -- Draft content structured for LinkedIn's profile sections

### Choosing a Template

CareerCanvas includes **68+ templates** organized by category:

- **Professional** -- Clean, traditional layouts
- **Technical** -- Optimized for engineering and tech roles
- **Creative** -- Modern designs with color and visual flair
- **ATS-Friendly** -- Simple formats that pass applicant tracking systems
- **Cover Letter** -- Purpose-built letter layouts
- **References** -- Dedicated reference sheet designs

To change templates: use the **Template** dropdown in the editor toolbar, or visit the **Template Studio** from the dashboard for full-screen browsing with live previews and side-by-side comparison.

### Page Size Options

Choose from A4, US Letter, US Legal, or A5 in the toolbar or Settings. Your choice is saved per document.

## 3. The Editor

### Three-Panel Layout

The editor is divided into three panels:

- **Left Panel** -- Form fields where you enter your information (personal details, sections, bullet points)
- **Center Panel** -- Live preview that updates as you type, showing exactly how your document will look when printed
- **Right Panel** -- Writing tips, completion progress bar, grammar check, and smart suggestions toggle

> 💡 **Tip:** Drag the dividers between panels to resize them. The left panel can range from 200px to 600px, and the right panel from 200px to 500px.

### Toolbar

The toolbar at the top of the editor provides quick access to:

- **Document name** -- Click to rename your document
- **Template selector** -- Switch templates instantly
- **Page size selector** -- Change between A4, Letter, etc.
- **ATS Mode** -- Toggle ATS-friendly formatting
- **Smart Format** -- AI-powered formatting analysis
- **Undo / Redo** -- Step through your edit history
- **Import** -- Bring in content from Word, PDF, JSON, or plain text files
- **Export** -- Save your document in multiple formats

### Personal Information

At the top of the left panel, fill in your details:

- Full name, job title, email, phone, location
- LinkedIn URL, portfolio/website, and GitHub

These fields appear in the header of your document and update the preview in real time.

### Working with Sections

**Adding sections:** Click the "+ Add Section" button at the bottom of the left panel. Choose from preset types (Experience, Education, Skills, Projects, etc.) or create a custom section.

**Editing items:** Each section contains items (jobs, degrees, skills, etc.). Click an item to expand it and edit its fields. Bullet points support rich text.

**Reordering sections:** Drag sections by their handle to reorder them. The preview updates immediately.

**Collapsing sections:** Click the section header to collapse or expand it, keeping your workspace tidy.

**Section visibility:** Toggle the eye icon on any section to hide it from the printed document without deleting it. Useful for tailoring your resume to different jobs.

**Section search and filter:** When you have many sections, use the search bar and filter buttons above them to find what you need quickly.

## 4. AI Features

CareerCanvas includes **18 AI-powered features** to help you write better content faster. AI features work out of the box when the server is configured, or you can add your own Groq or Gemini API key in Settings.

> 💡 **Tip:** AI only sends your resume text when you explicitly click an AI button. Nothing is sent automatically.

### How to Use Each AI Feature

1. **Improve Bullet** -- Click the sparkle button next to any achievement bullet point. AI rewrites it with stronger action verbs and quantified results.

2. **AI Generate Bullets** -- In any Experience or Project section, click "AI Generate." AI creates 5-6 tailored bullet points based on your job title and company.

3. **AI Summary** -- In the Professional Summary section, click "AI Summary." Generates a polished 2-3 sentence professional summary from your full resume.

4. **AI Suggest Skills** -- In the Skills section, click "AI Suggest Skills." Analyzes your experience and recommends relevant skills you may have missed.

5. **Check Grammar** -- In the right sidebar, click "Check Grammar." Scans your entire document for spelling, grammar, and style issues.

6. **AI Write Cover Letter** -- When editing a Cover Letter document, click "AI Write Cover Letter." Generates a complete cover letter based on your resume and the target job.

7. **AI Fix Suggestions** -- In the ATS Checker sidebar, after running an ATS scan, click "AI Fix" next to any issue. AI suggests specific fixes to improve your ATS score.

8. **Smart Format > AI Analyze** -- In the toolbar, click "Smart Format." AI analyzes your document's formatting consistency and suggests improvements.

9. **Smart Format > AI Summary** -- Within the Smart Format panel, click "AI Summary" to generate an executive summary of formatting issues found.

10. **AI Rewrite** -- Select any text in a bullet point to see the floating toolbar. Click the AI button to rewrite the selected text with improved phrasing.

11. **Interview Prep** -- In the Application Tracker, click "Interview Prep" on any tracked application. AI generates likely interview questions and suggested answers based on the job and your resume.

12. **Draft Email** -- In the Application Tracker, click "Draft Email" to generate a follow-up or thank-you email for a tracked application.

13. **Import from LinkedIn** -- In the Master Profile, click "Import from LinkedIn." Paste your LinkedIn profile text and AI parses it into structured resume sections.

14. **Improve All Bullets** -- In the Master Profile, click "Improve All Bullets." AI rewrites every bullet point across your profile for stronger impact.

15. **AI from Job Description** -- In the onboarding wizard, select "AI from Job Description." Paste a job posting and AI generates a complete starter resume matched to that role.

16. **AI Enhancement** -- In the Job Description Matcher results, click "AI Enhancement" to get AI-powered suggestions for better aligning your resume to the target job.

17. **AI Condense** -- In the Space Optimizer tool, click "AI Condense" to shorten verbose bullet points while preserving key information, helping your resume fit on fewer pages.

18. **AI Translate** -- In the Localization Studio, click "AI Translate" to translate your entire resume into another language while maintaining professional tone and formatting.

## 5. Templates

### Changing Templates

You can change templates at any time without losing content:

- **Quick switch:** Use the Template dropdown in the editor toolbar
- **Full browse:** Open the Template Studio from the dashboard for category filters, live previews, and side-by-side comparison of two templates

### Template Categories

Templates are grouped into: Professional, Technical, Creative, ATS-Friendly, Cover Letter, and References. Filter by column layout (one or two columns), font style (serif or sans-serif), or photo support.

### Photo Support

Some templates include a photo placeholder. To add your photo:

- Look for templates marked with photo support in the Template Studio
- Upload your photo in the Design Studio settings
- Choose photo shape (circle, square, rounded) and size

### Design Studio

For fine-grained customization beyond templates, open the Design Studio (accessible from the editor). You can adjust:

- **Typography** -- Font family, sizes for name/headings/body, line height
- **Colors** -- Accent color, text color, secondary text color, background
- **Section style** -- Heading style (bold, underline, uppercase), divider style, bullet style
- **Layout** -- Header alignment, contact layout, date alignment, sidebar position and width
- **Spacing** -- Section spacing, paragraph spacing, bullet spacing
- **Advanced** -- Icons, link style, page numbering, headers/footers

## 6. Import and Export

### Importing Documents

**From the editor toolbar:**
Click "Import" and choose a format:
- **Word Document (.docx)** -- Extracts text and structure from Word files
- **PDF Document (.pdf)** -- Parses text content from PDFs
- **CareerCanvas JSON (.json)** -- Full-fidelity import of previously exported documents
- **Plain Text (.txt, .md, .html)** -- Imports raw text content

**From the dashboard:**
Drag and drop any supported file directly onto the dashboard.

### Import Review Wizard

After importing a non-JSON file, the Import Review Wizard helps you refine the result:

- **Confidence indicators** -- Colored dots show how confident the parser is about each extracted section
- **Section reorder** -- Drag sections into the correct order
- **Merge** -- Combine sections that were incorrectly split
- **Duplicate detection** -- Flags content that appears to be duplicated
- **Edit before saving** -- Review and correct any field before creating the document
- **Draft auto-save** -- Your import progress is saved automatically so you can come back if interrupted

### Exporting Documents

Click "Export" in the toolbar and choose a format:

- **PDF** -- Opens your browser's print dialog with optimized print styles. Save as PDF from there.
- **JSON** -- Full CareerCanvas format for backup or transfer between browsers
- **Markdown (.md)** -- Clean text format for pasting into emails or other tools
- **HTML** -- Standalone HTML file with all styling included
- **Plain Text (.txt)** -- Simple text version of your document

### Printing

Press **Ctrl+P** (or Cmd+P on Mac) at any time, or use Export > PDF. CareerCanvas applies special print styles to ensure your document looks perfect on paper.

## 7. Tools

CareerCanvas includes several built-in tools accessible from the dashboard and editor.

### Job Description Matcher

Paste a job description and CareerCanvas compares it against your resume. See which keywords and skills match, which are missing, and get a match score. Use "AI Enhancement" for specific improvement suggestions.

### Skills Matrix

Visualize your skills across categories with proficiency levels. Identify gaps and see how your skill set aligns with industry expectations.

### Experience Calculator

Calculates your total professional experience in years and months. Breaks down experience by role, industry, and skill area.

### ATS Checker and ATS Mode

- **ATS Checker** -- Analyzes your resume for applicant tracking system compatibility. Checks formatting, keyword usage, section structure, and file parsing.
- **ATS Mode** -- Toggle in the toolbar to switch your document to a simplified, ATS-safe format. Removes complex formatting that might confuse automated parsers.

### Application Tracker

Track your job applications in one place:
- Add applications with company, role, status, and dates
- Track status (Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected, etc.)
- Use "Interview Prep" for AI-generated practice questions
- Use "Draft Email" for follow-up email drafts

### Studio Tools

Access these specialized tools from the editor and dashboard:

- **Theme Studio** -- Switch between light and dark themes, customize accent colors
- **Design Studio** -- Fine-tune typography, colors, spacing, and layout (see Section 5)
- **Section Studio** -- Manage section types, add custom sections, reorder defaults
- **PDF Studio** -- Configure PDF export settings and page layout
- **Font Manager** -- Browse and apply different font combinations
- **Space Optimizer** -- Identify ways to reduce page count (margins, spacing, AI Condense)
- **Localization Studio** -- Translate your resume into other languages with AI

## 8. Settings

Access Settings from the dashboard navigation.

### Appearance

- **Theme** -- Choose Light, Dark, or System (follows your OS preference)
- **Default Page Size** -- Set the default for new documents (A4, Letter, Legal, A5)
- **Preview Zoom** -- Set default zoom level (Fit Width, 75%, 100%, 125%, 150%)

### Editor

- **Autosave** -- Automatically saves your changes as you type (enabled by default)
- **Show Character Counts** -- Display character and word counts on text fields

### AI Configuration

- Add your own **Groq** or **Gemini** API key for AI features if the hosted server is unavailable
- AI features work without a personal key when using the hosted version

### Data Management

- **Export all data** -- Download all documents as a JSON backup
- **Import data** -- Restore from a previous backup
- **Clear all data** -- Delete all documents and settings from your browser
- **Storage info** -- See how much browser storage your documents use

## 10. Privacy and Data

### Local-First Storage

All your documents and settings are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded to any server unless you explicitly use an AI feature.

### AI Data Handling

- AI features send your resume text to the AI provider (Groq or Gemini) **only when you click an AI button**
- No data is sent automatically or in the background
- The AI provider processes your text and returns suggestions -- it does not store your data

### No Account Required

All core features work without creating an account. You can create, edit, export, and print documents with zero sign-up.

### Data Portability

Your data belongs to you. Export individual documents or all your data at any time using JSON export. Import them into any other browser where CareerCanvas is running.

> 💡 **Tip:** Regularly export a JSON backup of your documents. Since data lives in your browser, clearing browser data will remove your documents.
